# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

온기길 (Ongigil) is a public good built to help elderly people who collect recyclable paper for a living.
Everyone who takes part — contributors, users, helpers, community partners — deserves a welcoming, safe,
and respectful space. We promise to make participation free of harassment for people of every age,
ability, background, income, health, gender, ethnicity, religion, or level of tech experience.

## Why kindness matters here more than usual

This project serves **vulnerable elderly users**, often with little income, tired eyes, and little
comfort with technology. So our standards are a little higher than a typical open-source project:

- **Dignity is non-negotiable.** We never speak of the people we serve — or of each other — with pity,
  condescension, or contempt. We frame our work as neighbours helping neighbours, never as charity.
- **Privacy is care.** Protecting where people are and where they live is part of respecting them. Do not
  ask for, share, or handle personal or location data carelessly — in issues, PRs, or discussions.
- **Accessibility is respect.** Dismissing accessibility needs (large text, voice, simplicity) is not
  acceptable. Feedback from or about elderly users is treated as a gift.

## Expected behaviour

- Be warm, patient, and plain-spoken. Assume good faith.
- Welcome newcomers and non-coders; accessibility and wording feedback are first-class contributions.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully, focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's precise location, home, or identity.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Demeaning, pitying, or mocking language about elderly people, low-income people, or any user group.
- Publishing others' private information (including precise locations) without consent.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome attention.
- Sustained disruption, or advocating any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and in public
spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, or a privacy concern involving a person's data,
please report it privately to the maintainers at **conduct@clsoftlab.net**. For security or
location-privacy vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will listen, respect the reporter's safety and privacy, and
respond as promptly as we can.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour they judge harmful — with special weight given to conduct that endangers the dignity, safety,
or privacy of the elderly users this project serves. We aim to be fair, proportionate, and kind.
